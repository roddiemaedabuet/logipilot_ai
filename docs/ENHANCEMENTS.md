# Locked future enhancements — OUT OF CORE POC SCOPE

Never silently implement as a required POC feature. These are ideas for a later phase of the product roadmap, not interview commitments.

## P1 / shared platform
- **AI Quality Assurance & Coaching**: separate retrospective review of call transcripts and email conversations **one week after** interactions; scoring, checklist and coaching. Not folded into P1.
- Enterprise **Active Directory / Microsoft Entra ID SSO** and richer enterprise RBAC (initial POC uses admin-created credentials and per-module permissions).

## P2
- **P2-06**: demand forecasting from historical trends (core demand is confirmed bookings only).
- **P2-08**: external container leasing when internal options cannot meet deadlines.
- **P2-26**: full port restrictions, terminal capabilities, customs requirements and movement cutoff verification.
- **P2-29**: manager analytics dashboard of shortage, efficiency, repositioning expense, booking delays, and recommendation acceptance rates.
- **P2-31**: combine containers from multiple source depots/ports into one destination plan.
- **P2-32**: automatic revised recommended plan after approval and mandatory fresh approval. Do not confuse with pre-approval event-based reassessment (core).
- **P2-33**: proactive automatic notifications and alerts.
- **P2-36**: automatic reallocation on customer booking amendments (pickup date, quantity, container specs); distinct from core booking cancellation trigger.
- **P2-43**: AI recommendation confidence scoring.

## P3
- **Vessel Disruption Recovery**: original process concept, moved to enhancement by explicit decision.
- **P3-17**: AI-driven exception resolution or escalation recommendation for unresolved discrepancies (core uses manual Receiver intervention only).
- **P3-22**: formal exception case workflow for unresolved discrepancies (core records manual handling and blocks incomplete handoffs).
- Advanced cross-entity operational coordination and direct partner/terminal APIs; requires permissions, data agreements and actual integration. Tracking partner handoffs through manually/synthetically entered records is core.

## Explicit non-goals, not promises of enhancement
- No actual live enterprise integration or shipping network control in interview POC.
- No guaranteed delivery date, cost estimates based on actual rates, customs approval or terminal booking control.
- No automated sending of customer emails, container repositioning dispatch or unsupervised handoff completion in the POC.
